How to Stroke a Path for Uniform Line Weight

Stroking a path with a brush allows digital artists and designers to create perfectly smooth, consistent lines that are impossible to draw freehand. This guide outlines the exact process for setting up your brush to eliminate dynamic pressure variations, drawing your vector path, and applying the stroke to achieve an unvarying line weight throughout your artwork in raster-based editing software like Adobe Photoshop or GIMP.

1. Configure the Brush for Consistency

To guarantee a uniform line weight, you must disable any settings that dynamically alter the brush's thickness based on stylus pressure or speed.

  • Select the Brush Tool (B).
  • Open the Brush Settings panel (F5 in Photoshop, or the Tool Options in GIMP).
  • Deselect Shape Dynamics entirely. If you keep it enabled, ensure that Size Jitter and Control are set to Off rather than "Pen Pressure."
  • Choose a standard round brush tip.
  • Adjust the Hardness to 100% for crisp edges, or lower it slightly if you need a softer appearance.
  • Set the exact pixel size you want for your final line width.

2. Draw Your Path

With your brush prepared, outline the shape you want to trace using vector tools.

  • Create a new layer in your Layers panel so the stroked pixels remain separate from your background or sketch.
  • Select the Pen Tool (P).
  • Ensure the tool mode in the top options bar is set to Path, not "Shape."
  • Click and drag to create your anchor points and curves. Adjust the path handles until the curve matches your desired shape.

3. Apply the Stroke Along the Path

Once the path is set, apply the brush parameters directly onto it.

  • Open the Paths panel (found under Window > Paths).
  • Right-click on the active path layer (often labeled "Work Path") and select Stroke Path...
  • In the pop-up dialog, choose Brush from the Tool drop-down menu.
  • Crucially, ensure the Simulate Pressure checkbox is unchecked. Leaving this unchecked forces the software to render the line at the exact brush diameter from start to finish without tapering.
  • Click OK.

4. Finalize the Line

After applying the stroke, the vector outline will remain visible over your new pixels. Press Enter (or Return) while the Pen Tool is active, or click into an empty area of the Paths panel to deselect the path. Your line is now rendered on your active layer with an even, continuous weight.
